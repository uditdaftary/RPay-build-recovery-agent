Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026 · Track 03

An AI agent for Indian B2B receivables that decides — per debtor, not per invoice — whether to wait, collect, negotiate, reconcile, or escalate.

Measured recovery with a comprehensive audit trail. Built for the nuances of MSME guidelines and India-specific compliance.

■
Restraint
The agent knows when NOT to act. WAIT is treated as a first-class strategy. Intelligent suppression prevents unnecessary friction.
◈
India Correctness
TDS reconciliation, MSMED s.15/16, Section 43B(h), Udyam eligibility gate. The agent strictly avoids citing statutes when the merchant is ineligible.
▲
Counterfactual
Evaluated against a baseline on an identical seed. Real, measured delta in recovery performance, not fabricated metrics.
